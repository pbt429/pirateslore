New to the bells? Old Tom, the Swallow's gray-haired bosun, will drill you on deck first. He calls out each blow, then swings slower than your rival ever will, and speeds up as you get the hang of it.

Starting moves you into the duel area. With JAWS or NVDA it switches to application mode by itself so your keys go to the game. Press H at any time for the keys.

On a phone or tablet, the fight is played with the big buttons under the deck. There are three rows: high at the top, middle, and low at the bottom. Each row goes Parry, Thrust, Slash, then Back. With VoiceOver or TalkBack, slide one finger to find a button and double tap. The game starts you on Parry middle.

Keys

Your rival always warns you before he strikes. You hear a bell: a quick high double ring means high, one middle ring means middle, a low buzz means low. A rushing whoosh before the bell means a slash. Parry at the same height to block him, then strike back while he recovers.

Phones and tablets

Under the deck are three rows of big buttons: high at the top, middle, and low at the bottom. Each row goes Parry, Thrust, Slash, and Back, which parries and steps back. Below them are Step back, Step forward, Status and Pause. Sighted players just tap. With VoiceOver or TalkBack, slide one finger over the rows to find a button, then double tap anywhere. Because the buttons never move, you can learn where they are, the way you learn keys. Choose Relaxed or Very relaxed fight speed to give yourself time. A Bluetooth keyboard or controller also works.

If JAWS keeps the number pad for itself, use the letter keys instead. Every hit you land lifts your crew's morale and lowers his. When a fighter's morale runs out, the next hit makes him yield.
