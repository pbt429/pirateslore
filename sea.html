Keys

Steering works in compass points. There are 16 points around the compass, so one press of an arrow turns you about the width of a clock's hour hand. Your speed depends on the angle between your heading and the wind. Straight into the wind you can only row, and slowly. With the wind behind your shoulder, the Swallow flies.

What you hear: waves all around, wind that grows with the breeze, and surf that gets louder and moves left or right as you near a coast. A ship in sight sends a soft tick from her direction, lower when she is behind you. A fast double tick means she is chasing you. The guide beacon chimes from your destination's direction and rings high and doubled when you are pointed right at it. In a strong wind a gust can knock your bow one point off course, and dark squalls bring rain, thunder and more gusts. The game tells you which way to turn to get back on course.
