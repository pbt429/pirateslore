The Spanish Main, 1560 to 1697

Pirates Lore

A pirate's life in the Caribbean, built from the start to be played by ear.

Win your first ship in a sword fight. Then sail where you like: trade, take prizes, sack Spanish towns, court the governor's daughter, hunt for buried treasure and your lost family, and chase the Treasure Fleet. When you're old and rich, or broke and marooned, the game tells you how your life turned out.

Start a new careerChoose your nation, time period and skill, then win the Swallow in a duel. Continue your voyagePick up where you left off. Built for screen readers

Every sight on screen is also a sound or a spoken line. Made and tested with JAWS, and works with NVDA and VoiceOver.

Play your way

Keyboard, touch, mouse, or a game controller such as the 8BitDo M30. Headphones help: ships and coasts sound from their direction.

Your game is saved

Your voyage saves itself in this browser on this device, so you can stop and come back.
